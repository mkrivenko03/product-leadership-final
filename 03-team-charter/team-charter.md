# Team Charter: Meridian Foundations

> Module 3 · Set the Habits That Make a Team Faster

## 1. What We Own

> **We own:** The Foundations initiative and its outcomes: sustained field adoption, reporting completeness, and trustworthy project status information. PM 1 owns field adoption and the mobile experience, PM 2 owns the enterprise experience supporting Foundations outcomes, and the Foundations Product Lead is accountable for protecting the quarterly bets and their allocated engineering capacity.
>
> **Out of scope:** Renewal-specific enterprise features that do not directly support Foundations outcomes belong to PM 2’s broader enterprise roadmap and must be funded separately. Mid-market expansion, additional field workflows, and advanced inference capabilities remain outside the current quarter’s scope until explicitly approved for investment.
>
> **Joint calls:** Any decision that reallocates shared engineering capacity, changes quarterly success targets, or introduces dependencies between field and enterprise roadmaps requires prior approval from the Foundations Product Lead, both PMs, and the Engineering Lead. Sales-driven commitments cannot be made before their impact on existing bets is assessed and approved.

## 2. How We Decide

> **Who decides scope:** PM 1 and PM 2 independently decide feature scope, prioritization, and trade-offs within their respective domains, provided they do not compromise agreed quarterly outcomes or capacity allocations. The Foundations Product Lead has final decision authority over changes to strategic bets, cross-team priorities, and engineering capacity allocation.
>
> **How cross-team conflicts escalate:** If PM 1 and PM 2 cannot resolve a conflict, either PM must escalate it to the Foundations Product Lead within one business day through a written decision brief outlining competing priorities, business impact, engineering capacity required, KR risks, and recommended options. The Foundations Product Lead makes and communicates the binding final decision within two business days of escalation. Until that decision is documented, existing engineering allocations remain unchanged, and neither PM nor Sales may make commitments that compromise the field adoption bet.
